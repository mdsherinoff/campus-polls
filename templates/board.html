How the board protects the votes

In a protected poll each entry holds the encrypted ballot, the SHA-256 hash of the previous entry, its own hash (the voter's receipt) and the server's Ed25519 signature. Changing any ballot, or the order of ballots, breaks the chain. The server's public signing key is published at /api/board-public-key.pem so anyone can check the signatures independently, for example with OpenSSL.
